AC/DC indicator

Mark V and A readings as DC or AC next to the unit. „Symbol“ uses the IEC marks (⏃ / ∿) – switch to Tilde or Text if they don’t render on your device.

Help

The small ⓘ icons next to controls open a short explanation when tapped. Turn them off here to hide them everywhere.

The device is always reachable at owon.local (fixed). A static IP is guaranteed – pick a free address outside the DHCP range. Changes take effect after reboot.

Unit

„Match meter“: the unit follows the measurement range (e.g. 5 V range → shown in V) – just like the multimeter display.
„Auto-scale“: nicest unit for the value (mV/V/kV …).

Sound

Plays a tone while continuity is detected (below the threshold set on the Continuity page), like the multimeter buzzer. Browser audio plays from this device; with the second option the OWON’s own buzzer is switched along with the toggle.

Behavior

When on, the firmware reads the meter’s function (FUNC?) about every 2 s, so turning the rotary on the device makes the web UI follow. Costs one query per cycle → slightly fewer samples/s. (Temperature can’t be detected this way – the device doesn’t report it.)

Maintenance

Erases WiFi & network settings and reboots into the setup access point.
